# SSH connection result delivery

## Scope

The SSH service can adopt a connector before registration, startup, or a synchronous cancellation callback fails. The bridge must preserve that owner's actual connection result, including an uncertain durable write, without treating a completed future as proof that native cleanup finished. This is a local ownership and delivery contract; it does not establish complete Android lifecycle or started-connector cancellation behavior.

## Ownership and ordering

`SshSessionService.attach` publishes the new `SshSessionOwner` and binds its lifecycle to that exact owner before `startPublished` installs the connector's cancellation action. Binding may synchronously call back, throw, or encounter an unconfirmed native invalidation. Startup failures after publication must return the same `owner.connected` future, and cleanup stays with that owner. An unknown native invalidation is retained and is not repeated as if confirmed. Service startup and a Stop are ordered by the shared nonblocking admission; work admitted first may finish after Stop.

An admitted factory which may write trust remains in its creating/settlement state until either its terminal is installed under the owner monitor or the outcome accounting for its failure and applicable deferred-result rules is fixed on the connection future under that monitor. Screen lock or service rejection must not complete the future in between factory exit and settlement. Preserve an actual `VAULT_COMMIT_UNCERTAIN` outcome. Keep the first shared Stop reason and the first applicable deferred result as separate facts: tracked credential saving retains its existing exceptions, so the eventual result is not necessarily the first Stop code. A factory which never entered may settle a Stop result even if its cleanup is unconfirmed. The resulting future and owner release are separate events.

## Bridge completion

The actual `whenComplete` callback queues a completion through the main dispatcher. A per-attempt atomic guard admits completion once; a separate guard is set before invoking `MethodChannel.Result`, because a channel call can throw after delivery. Do not retry a possibly delivered reply. State-event, connector cancellation and vault-lock exceptions must not replace an already selected result or skip this reply attempt. If `Result` throws before sending, the peer may receive nothing; if the dispatcher accepts a task but its Looper exits, it may never execute. If the main dispatcher rejects a completion, the callback can attempt a failure on its own thread and retains its bridge attempt instead of mutating main-thread bridge state.

On unsuccessful completion, a reported uncertain trust receipt is preserved. If a credential write is known to have occurred and the subsequent outcome is cancellation, vault failure, timeout, screen lock or service-start rejection, report `VAULT_COMMIT_UNCERTAIN`, including if the subsequent vault lock throws. Do not infer a committed write from an attempted operation. Use the connector's unstarted retirement operation rather than a separate `started()` snapshot; it never releases a separately adopted service owner. If a nonconforming attacher throws after its factory entered and supplies no future, do not invent an in-flight receipt or claim cleanup; the production service must return its original future.

When a new admission sees the screen already locked and an older service owner exists, an exact shared invalidation failure during that owner's stop may be converted to the new request's `SSH_SCREEN_LOCKED` only after the old owner's cleanup-failure handling runs and no additional event or scheduling step failed. Preserve other errors, the original owner and any in-flight trust receipt. The ordinary owner stop entry retains its existing failure behavior.

## Checks and limits

Cover synchronous cancellation during binding, service failure before and after factory admission, successful terminal transfer, and the gap between factory exit and result settlement. Exercise first-stop state, tracked-save deferred outcomes and the uncertain outcome. A host fixture may pause a synthetic post-create handoff to test the same settlement state, but must not describe that as a real store write.

Exercise the actual bridge callback and a Result recorder with a controlled FIFO dispatcher: duplicate completions, exceptions before/after channel reply, failed events, cancellation, lock, first Stop reason, known write/no-write mappings, and a blocked factory's exact result. JNI fault hooks should run in the thread that performs the tested invalidation; thread-local probes do not prove another thread made no JNI call. Host tests bypassing Android construction or using fake factories do not prove Android Handler, receiver registration, a real trust store, or device lifecycle behavior.

The uncertain-result path now retires the exact published preview under the separate [vault contract](ssh-vault-revocation-ownership.md); an active import or native drain may delay physical cleanup. Selected started material installations follow their own [STOP admission contract](ssh-started-material-admission.md). Mailbox installation and native credential effects remain separate. A standalone host JNI permit probe shows a window after Java STOP publication but before native invalidation; it does not demonstrate an actual SSH packet. An independently active vault lease or native resource is not proven revoked merely by a bridge result or local Stop. First-use trust, reconnect and complete chain cleanup retain their separate gates.
